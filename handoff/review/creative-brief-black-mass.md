# Creative brief: big black masses + continuously mixed oil layouts (2026-09-25)

From the creative-director chat. It gives targets and acceptance only; key names and code are for
the implementing chat. Approved by the user as the next priority after request 5. Evidence:
`colour-mocks/layouts.png` (rough mock) and the user's panel photos in `proven/`.

## Why
The proven photos carry 20-30% black oil; the app today runs ~9%. The black contrast is what makes
the proven looks work. Lower APL also helps ABL.

## Layout points (presets = the means; the clocks glide between them)
| Layout | Black % | Shape | Time weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Few giants | ~28 | 2-3 huge lobed masses, some cut by the frame edge; sparse droplets | proven, 40% |
| Lava rise | ~25 | tall blobs rising; small bubbles pinch off the tops | proven, 30% |
| Today | ~9 | one medium mass + scattered droplets | moderate, 12% |
| Starfield | ~12 | dense small droplets only, no big mass | moderate, 8% |
| Rafts | ~8 | droplets gathered in rings around a small core | wild, 10% |

- The weights follow 7:2:1 (proven : moderate : wild).
- User: "all of them, approx those splits, do continuous mixing of all of them."
- EXISTING KEYS FIRST (the user ruled no new features for episodes; treat that spirit here too). A
  layout that existing keys can't reach, likely rafts, gets a cost note and goes to the user; it
  does not get built quietly.

## Motion
- Continuous mixing on the per-key clocks (the distribution model). There are no sharp layout
  cuts; a cut only happens if the ~7-min look cut lands there.
- The masses must move and evolve (rise, merge, pinch off). A parked static blob is a fail.

## Rules that still hold
Oil and droplets stay pure black (no dye, no glow bodies); droplet fields are never "bubbly";
the second-colour patches still read at photo size next to the bigger black areas.

## Acceptance (one contact sheet)
- Per layout: an app render at 1280x720 plus its black % measured by coverage readback (mean of
  60 s), within +-5 points of the target.
- One row of the proven photos alongside for comparison.
- A 2-3 min mix clip or a 6-frame strip showing a glide between two layouts.
- HDR on and off.
