1. `oil_transparency` (0..1): the oil becomes an absorbing film over the REFRACTED ink instead of a flat fill. Physically: transmitted = inkRefracted * T, T = exp(-k * thickness * (1 - oilColorNormalised)) (Beer-Lambert per channel: an orange film passes red/green, absorbs blue), plus a scatter/emission term = oilColor * (1 - T_avg) so thick oil still reads as the oil colour when the ink is black (backlit dish: light comes THROUGH the ink layer). Thickness = the same proxy the edge work uses (saturate(sdf/edgeW) or field-based) times a low-frequency fbm bump so the film has islands. 2. `oil_refract_body` (uv units, default 0): refraction across the WHOLE oil body, not only the edge band — offset the ink lookup by the gradient of (field + fbm bump) so marbling seen through the oil wobbles; keep the existing edge refraction as is.
3. `oil_ink_blur` (0..1): the ink seen through the film is slightly out of focus (refs): a small 4-tap blur of the refracted ink sample, weighted by thickness.
4. Interaction with the edge keys: thin edges already darken toward the ink; with transparency on, the thin edge should naturally be the most transparent part (T ~ 1 at t=0). Make sure holes (negative-weight blobs) and swarm droplets still read correctly (holes show pure ink, no film).
