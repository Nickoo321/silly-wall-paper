# Quiz — evaluation round (run when you're back, ~15 min)

Protocol: for each look, apply it (Looks window or Moods menu), give it ~90s
of fresh paint, then answer the questions OUT LOUD to me. HDR on for the
first pass; we repeat key ones with HDR off (Win+Alt+B) at the end.
Screenshots lie about darks — trust your eyes only.

## 1. Journeys (the director)

- **Journey Stoner (gold v2)**: does each leg hold ONE family? Are the 20s
  blends the good part? Does gold lead the palette enough? Any ugly zone?
- **Journey Magenta**: is this the magenta you approved, preserved right?
- **Journey Duet**: can you SEE the model working — old family rotated into
  the new one's partner color (blue<->purple) while emission stays clean?
- **Dark intermissions** (all journeys): do they feel intentional, or like
  the wallpaper died? Too long/short (currently 20s)?
- **Pacing**: glides are 12s, blends 20s, dwells 35-45s — what drags?

## 2. Static moods (flagships)

For each: is the hue family right, and is anything out-of-band (green
creep, yellow shift, neon leak)?
- Ember Giant (your praised dark-red-bordering-orange zone)
- Solar Wind (gold)  - Verdant (green — your known hate zone, judge fairly)
- Glacier (pale cyan)- Abyss (deep blue, dark)  - Pluto (cream/tan)
- Violet Front       - Clouds / Storm (already hand-tuned by you)

## 3. Texture sanity (per look)

- Marbling inside big masses: present, fine, not stringy, not blocky?
- Dark regions: marbled gray (floor on) or dead black (floor off) — which
  reads better per mood?
- Any straight jet lines, ×10 sun-orbs, or dotty noise? (all should be gone)

## 4. Housekeeping

- Settings window: paged nav OK? Find things? Tooltips help?
- Mood 1-6 auto-files: keep or delete?
- HDR off check (Win+Alt+B): 3 favorite looks — still good, no crush/wash?

Results go into CALIBRATION.md as final sign-offs (or re-tune notes).
