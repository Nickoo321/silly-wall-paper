## 2. Shader literal budget (MSVC cap 16380 bytes)

24 literals, none over cap. Three largest:

| bytes | headroom | lines | literal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14081 | **2299** | 1089-1316 | `kDisplaySrc` |
| 13609 | 2771 | 3185-3414 | `kPostSrc` |
| 13582 | 2798 | 9-302 | `kComputeSrc` |

2299 bytes is roughly 30 lines of this project's commented HLSL. The chunk that overflows first is
the one nobody is currently editing, so the failure will arrive as a surprise build break in
whatever branch touches it next. Splitting the top three pre-emptively is a 10-minute rote job.

## 3. The ring competition

Every term that paints the droplet boundary, with the live amplitude in `acid-rise-12.ini` and the
fixed inner constant the shader multiplies it by. "Peak" = the two multiplied.

| term | key | ini | shader factor | peak | acts on | width source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| meniscus halo | `meniscus` | 0.85 | `lerp(col, menC, w)` | **0.85** | replaces the pixel | `meniscus_width` 0.0020 |
| dark rim | `rim_dark` | 0.80 | `oilC *= 1-k*rimB` | 0.80 | darkens film | `rim_width` 0.0016 |
| swarm lens | `swarm_lens` | 0.80 | fringe 0.75 / ring 0.55 | 0.60 | trapped droplets | 0.26 x droplet r |
| thin edge | `oil_thin_edge` | 0.70 | `lerp(oilC, prod, tt^2*k)` | 0.70 | film -> ink product | `oil_edge_frac` 0.14 |
| droplet lens | `droplet_lens` | 0.55 | dome .30/band .30/rim .30 | 0.17 | every droplet | `droplet_lens_band` 3 px |
| oil glow | `oil_glow` | 0.45 | `+oilThinC * 0.45` | 0.20 | ink outside | `edgeW * 1.3` |
| penumbra | `oil_penumbra` | 0.30 | hue rotate + darken | 0.30 | oil side only | `oil_penumbra_px` 8 |
| mass rim | `mass_rim` | 0.35 | `+tint * 0.28` | **0.098** | mass edge, lamp side | **hardcoded 3 px** |
| bright-field halo | `halo` | 0.12 | `+bright * 0.60` | **0.072** | brighter side | `halo_px` 12 |

Eight of the nine paint the same few pixels. The ordering is the finding: the **meniscus** replaces
up to 85% of the boundary pixel and, with `meniscus_from_ink = 1.0`, its colour is derived from the
INK, so it is structurally immune to every film-hue feature. The two terms that actually carry the
film's colour outward — `mass_rim` and the bright-field halo — peak at 0.098 and 0.072.

**Proposal for AF/AW and AJ.** AF ("weak lid reflections"), AW ("weak lid effect") and AJ are three
requests for more of the same ~0.17 of budget, and each will keep reading as "weak" while an
ink-coloured 0.85 sits on top of it. They should not be judged, or tuned, one key at a time. Before
another amplitude key is added: one sheet of `acid-rise-12` at fixed seed/time with each of the nine
terms **soloed** (the other eight at 0), so the user can see what each one is really contributing —
that sheet is rote and cheap. Then the real question is a single key, not a tenth term: should the
meniscus take a share of the FILM's colour (`meniscus_film_mix`) instead of the ink's? If yes, AF,
AW and AJ are all mostly answered by it and should be re-judged on the panel afterwards, not before.
Note also that `mass_rim` — the term the user calls weak — has its width **hardcoded** at 3 px while
the comment table advertises `rimW(uv)`, so there is no slider to turn up even if it were wanted.

## 4. EXECUTOR-CARD.md

| # | issue | severity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tools\gpu-lock.ps1` resolves the lock from **its own** repo root (`Split-Path -Parent $PSScriptRoot`). Dot-sourced from a worktree it locks `<worktree>\build2\shots\gpu.lock`, and `build2\` is gitignored — so **each worktree gets a private lock that excludes nothing**. With four branches in flight there are up to five independent "locks". The card does not say to use the MAIN repo's copy. Every concurrency guarantee the card claims is void unless executors happen to do what I did today. | **critical** |
| 2 | The lock rule was relaxed to "wait at most 2 min, then render anyway" and the rationale — `Never two --shot renders at once (2026-09-15: OLED went grey)` — was **deleted in the same edit**. The next executor cannot weigh a risk it can no longer see. The policy may well be what the user wants; the incident line should sit next to it, with what to do if the panel misbehaves. | **high** |
| 3 | Card mandates sending every sheet "via SendUserFile". No such tool is in the executor toolset. The rule is unactionable as written and silently skipped. | high |
| 4 | §3 says "Build with `cmd /c build-wt.cmd` in the worktree", but `build-wt.cmd` is in `.git\info\exclude`, so a fresh worktree does not have it. Every executor re-invents it (I did today). Either commit it or paste the cmake line into the card. | medium |
| 5 | Parity is defined only for `style=fluid`. There is no recipe for proving the ACID presets are byte-identical, which is the rule that actually bites on every acid key. I had to build a pristine-main worktree by hand. Recipe belongs in §3. | medium |
| 6 | Commit trailer says `Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5.1`. Actual practice on main is the model that did the work (Sonnet 5, Fable 5.1, Opus 5). Card should say "the model doing the work". | low |
| 7 | §4 "Current state" pins `main @ fd81f49`; main is `1ea3611`. §5 still lists AJ as "FUTURE, do not start". Both stale. | low |
